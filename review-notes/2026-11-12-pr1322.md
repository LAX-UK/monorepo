## What checks out
- **Same price computation.** The page price comes from `IReofferPricing.priceFor`. That function calls `invoiceRaiser.price` with the lot's hammer price replaced by the offer's amount (`create-reoffer-services.ts:89-99`), and the invoice raised after accept goes through the same `buildInvoiceLines`. Amounts are integer pence, and the schema checks that the total equals the sum of the lines. Once accepted, the page reads the stored invoice lines.
- **Offer at the bidder's own bid.** `amountPence` is the offer's own amount, never the hidden max.
- **Accept is only a signed-in POST.** It goes through a server action to `POST /legal-entities/reoffers/:id/accept`. The server requires `termsAccepted === true`: the zod literal plus `reoffer-response.service.ts:77`. Notifications open `/bids/offers/lot/:lotId`, which only redirects and never accepts.
- **Restricted, paused or closed accounts are refused on the server:** `accountState !== "active"` gives `bidder_unavailable`, and `moneyPause.assertNotPaused`. Role is checked by `requireMayAnswer` (`entity.bid.place`).
- **Needs-you is shown only to accounts that can bid:** `allowed: entity.bid.place && acting` plus `requires: "bid"`, so restricted accounts lose the `bid` action and the task. Stale offers are filtered out (`status = offered AND offerEndsAt > now`). The integration test covers send, end and decline.
- **The bidder view never names the seller or the buyer who did not pay.** The integration test checks the text for the seller and buyer ids. The new fields are only lot title, number, sale, image and end date.
- **Cross-account access.** The API's `findForBidderAccount` is scoped to the acting entity. The record-account hop switches only to accounts the person belongs to. Anyone else gets `/no-access` and is never told who owns the offer. This matches the existing purchase and consignment links. It is a no-access page, not a strict 404, but no data leaks.
- **Dates use `@auction/datetime` in London time,** and offers end at 17:00 London (domain `offerEndsAt`).
- **Code structure is clean:** a state table in the view model, one table for errors, and separate loader, view-model and component.

## Fix first
1. **A VAT line the invoice does not have.** `apps/web/src/lib/reoffer/bidder-reoffer.vm.ts:228` and `apps/api/src/services/reoffer/reoffer-reads.service.ts:121`
   - *Scenario:* the real pricer (`buildInvoiceLines`) only ever produces `hammer` and `buyers_premium` lines. So every offer page shows "VAT on the buyer's premium £0.00", next to a binding "I agree to buy this lot at the total shown" box. The invoice that follows has no VAT line. The page tells the buyer VAT is nil when the rules are not yet confirmed, and the page's lines do not match the invoice's lines. The unit fixture (`reoffer-bidder-view.test.ts`, `PRICED`) uses a `vat_on_premium` line that production never produces, so this is not caught.
   - *Fix:* make `vatOnPremiumMinor` nullable like the other optional lines. Return `null` when the priced lines have no `vat_on_premium` line, and render it with `optional(...)`. Add a test that uses `buildInvoiceLines` output.
2. **Acceptance is not tied to the total shown.** `reoffer-offer-answer.tsx:61` → `bidder-reoffer.ts` → `acceptReofferBodySchema` (`packages/validators/src/lot-reoffer.ts:74`)
   - *Scenario:* the bidder loads the page. Staff then change the sale's premium tiers or the lot's premium rate, and the bidder accepts. The invoice is priced fresh at accept and charges a total the bidder never saw, even though the box says "at the total shown".
   - *Fix:* send `expectedTotalMinor` (or a price token) with the accept. Under the lot lock, price again and refuse with a settled `price_changed` code if it differs. The page then reloads to show the new total.

## Follow-ups (not blocking)
3. **The invoice due date can differ from the stored one.** `reoffer-reads.service.ts:131` works out `invoiceDueAt` from the acceptance time (`respondedAt`). The stored invoice's `dueAt` is worked out from the time the invoice was raised. These differ when the raise fails and the backfill runs on a later day, or when accept and raise fall either side of London midnight. Read the stored due date from the live invoice when there is one.
4. **Contradictory purchase subtitle.** `purchases/[lotId]/page.tsx:111-118` produces "Bought after the auction at your bid · 12 Nov 2026 · won by Alex". Drop "won by" (or say "bought by") when `boughtOn` is set.
5. **An accepted offer that later defaulted still reads as bought.** If the new buyer also defaults and the lot is taken back, the view still shows "You bought this lot" (`bidder-reoffer.vm.ts:207`) with a purchase link that now gives no-access. Consider a state that says the purchase has ended.
6. **Slow purchase page.** `reofferBoughtOn` (`bidder-reoffer.server.ts`) runs on every purchase detail page, after the other reads. It lists and prices every offer one after another (about 3 queries each) only to read `respondedAt`. Run it alongside the other reads, or add a light endpoint that does no pricing.
7. **The lot hop gives up quietly.** `/bids/offers/lot/[lotId]` sends the person to Bids when the acting account has no offer for the lot. Inbox rows switch account first, so this only affects links opened in the wrong account. Accept as is, or fall back to the record-account hop.
