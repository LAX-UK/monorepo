## Fix 2: accept is tied to the total shown. Fixed.
- The schema is `acceptReofferBodySchema = { termsAccepted: literal(true), expectedTotalMinor: pence }.strict()`. A missing total, or a total sent as a string, is refused before any work (route test).
- `reoffer-response.service.ts:212-225`: inside the lot-locked transaction, after every other check and before `changeStatus`, reaward and the events, the lot is priced again with the same `IReofferPricing.priceFor` the page used. If the total differs, it throws `price_changed` (409). Nothing changes, no event is recorded and no invoice is raised, because the raise only runs after the transaction commits.
- A repeat accept (`alreadyAccepted`) returns before the price check. A double click after a successful accept therefore cannot be refused by a later price change. This is correct.
- The integration test `refuses an accept at a total that changed since the page loaded` changes `lot.buyerPremiumRate` to 0.30 between load and accept. It asserts `price_changed`, that the offer is still `offered`, that `respondedAt` is null, that there is no winner, that `control.invoiced` is empty and that there is no accepted or reawarded event. It then reloads (total 117,000, premium 27,000), accepts at the new total and checks that the invoice is raised.
- On the web, the component sends `vm.price.totalMinor`. On `price_changed` it unticks the box, shows a "The total has changed" warning, calls `router.refresh()` and does not navigate. `authedServerFetch` uses `no-store`, so the refresh brings back the new total, and that total becomes the next `expectedTotalMinor`. Covered by the action, component and error-table tests.

## Follow-ups (not blocking)
1. **The invoice is still priced separately after commit.** `create-reoffer-services.ts` `raiseFor` calls `lotInvoiceInitiationService.ensureForLot`, which prices again (`packages/finance-runtime/src/lot-invoice-initiation.service.ts:160`) after the accept transaction has committed. This leaves two gaps:
   - A short window between commit and raise.
   - If the raise fails, the backfill runs later.

   A premium change during either gap (for example from the ATG sync, `apps/worker/src/integrations/atg/drizzle-atg-sync-store.ts`, or from staff) would raise an invoice at a total the bidder did not agree to. Normal auction wins have the same model, so this is not a regression.

   *Fix:* store the agreed total on the offer row or on the `lot.reawarded` event. Have the raise for a re-awarded lot refuse, or alert staff, when its priced total differs from the agreed total.
2. **A refused line kind breaks the whole list.** `bidderPriceLines` throws on any line kind it cannot show (`import_vat`, `delivery`, `legacy_total`). `listForBidder` does not catch this, so one such invoice (for example a stored invoice that later gains a delivery line) would make the whole Bids offers list and the offer page fail for that account, not just that one offer. None of these kinds can appear today. When delivery or import VAT lines arrive, either map them or fail that one offer only.
3. The pay-page test is flaky under full-suite load (see above). It has nothing to do with this PR.
