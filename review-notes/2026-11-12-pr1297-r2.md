### F2 (MEDIUM): accept re-checks account standing under the lock. **Fixed**
- `apps/api/src/services/reoffer/reoffer-response.service.ts:185-197`: after `lockOwn` (lot `FOR UPDATE`, then offer) and the window/transition/consent checks, `workflow.placedBids(tx, lot.id)` finds the source bid. Accept is refused with `409 bidder_unavailable` unless the bid still exists, its entity is still the offered entity, and `accountState === "active"`. `accountState` (`drizzle-reoffer-workflow.reader.ts:24-42`) covers deletion pending, archived/merged, suspended, identity disabled, and entity `restricted`/`rejected`, so the buyer-default restriction is covered. This is the same predicate `sendLocked` uses (`reoffer-offer.service.ts:146-159`).
- The check runs before `changeStatus` and `reaward`, so a refusal writes nothing. Test `reoffer.integration.test.ts:983`: suspend → accept → `bidder_unavailable`, offer still `offered`, no winner change, no invoice. Passes.
- Lock order: `placedBids` is a plain SELECT with no row locks, so it adds no lock and cannot invert the order.
- Note (no action): the standing checked is the offered *user's* (the source bidder), not the acting teammate's. That matches who becomes `winnerId`. The acting user's own session standing is gated upstream.

## New observations (no blocker)
- **N1 (INFO): the register isn't re-asserted at re-award.** If the offer-lock projector processes a stale `lot.ended` *after* the buyer default (the lot is now `ended`, `hasWinner=false`), it moves the register to `withdrawn` (`artwork-offer-lock.ts:47-52`). Zoho then shows "Withdrawn" while a re-offer is pending or after the re-award, and Shop could offer the original elsewhere. The place guard would then refuse the accept (offered elsewhere), so the money side is safe. But after a successful re-award, nothing moves the register back to `bid_sale` for this lot. Suggested follow-up: have the re-award call `ArtworkOfferLock.offer(link, bid_lot:<lot>)` after commit, or make `bidOfferMove` treat a lot with an open or accepted re-offer as still offered. This depends on projector lag, so it's not a blocker.
