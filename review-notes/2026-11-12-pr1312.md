false for some lots (F1), and that should be fixed before the copy is wired. F2 is a gap the wiring
PR will hit. The rest are small.

## Findings

### F1. The offer email says "the seller has agreed" for LAX-owned lots too. **Medium.**
- **Where:** `packages/email/src/reoffer-copy.ts:80` (`offerMadeCopy`, first paragraph).
- **Scenario:** for LAX-owned stock no seller consent is asked (`packages/domain/src/reoffer-consent.ts:44-47`:
  `lax_owned` → `not_needed`), so the offer goes straight to the bidder. The bidder's binding offer
  email still says "...and the seller has agreed to sell it to you at £X". That is a false
  statement of fact in the email that sets out the binding terms, and the lawyer is meant to confirm
  that wording (see the file's own header comment).
- **Fix:** either use neutral wording for every lot ("...the lot is now offered to you at £X, your
  highest placed bid"), or add an `ownership: "seller" | "lax_owned"` var to `reoffer-offer-made`
  and branch. Add a render test for the LAX-owned wording. The same applies to the staff "accepted"
  line `reoffer-copy.ts:166` ("The seller has been told."). For LAX-owned stock there is no seller to
  tell, so make it conditional or drop it.

### F2. No seller reminder template. **Medium (blocks wiring, not this copy).**
- **Where:** the template list `packages/email/src/templates/types/reoffer.ts:5-13`.
- **Scenario:** the domain sends one seller reminder at 17:00 on the 2nd working day
  (`packages/domain/src/reoffer-windows.ts:56-64`, `reoffer-rules.ts:9-10`
  `SELLER_CONSENT_REMINDER_WORKING_DAYS = 2`). There is a bidder reminder but no
  `reoffer-seller-consent-reminder`. The wiring PR will either have no words for it or will resend
  the request email, which reads as a new request.
- **Fix:** add `reoffer-seller-consent-reminder` and `sellerConsentReminderCopy`, e.g. "We are still
  waiting for your answer... by 17:00 (London time) on ...". Or record in the PR that the design
  sends no seller email reminder.

### F3. "London time" is missing on the seller deadline and on every fact card. **Low.**
- **Where:** `reoffer-copy.ts:34-38` (`reofferDeadlineText`) and its callers:
  `reoffer-copy.ts:65` (seller consent paragraph), `templates/reoffer-notices.tsx:84,102,121`
  (the "Answer by" and "Offer ends" fact rows).
- **Scenario:** RA8 says windows end at "17:00 London". The bidder paragraphs add "(London time)"
  by hand. The seller's request ("Please tell us by 17:00 on 14 November 2026.") and all the fact
  rows do not. A seller or bidder abroad reads 17:00 in their own time.
- **Fix:** put the zone inside `reofferDeadlineText`, e.g. `17:00 (London time) on 15 November 2026`,
  and remove the hand-added "(London time)" from `:81` and `:93`. Then every surface (email, fact
  card, in-app) says it the same way. Extend the "states deadlines at London time" test to check the
  seller copy.

### F4. The in-app message for a new offer gives no deadline. **Low.**
- **Where:** `reoffer-copy.ts:21-25` ("The first [paragraph] is also the in-app message") and `:78-82`.
- **Scenario:** the in-app notice for `offerMadeCopy` is paragraph 0 only: the price but no end time
  and no "from your workspace". The deadline is the one thing the bidder must act on. The reminder's
  paragraph 0 does include it.
- **Fix:** move the deadline sentence into paragraph 0, or add an explicit `inApp` field to `ReofferNoticeCopy`.

### F5. Staff "withdrawn" sentence is ungrammatical. **Nit.**
- **Where:** `reoffer-copy.ts:162` + `:174`. Output: "Bob B had their offer withdrawn for the re-offer of
  Lot 12 at £1,200."
- **Fix:** use a per-outcome sentence, e.g. "The re-offer of Lot 12 to Bob B at £1,200 was withdrawn."

### F6. `price` is free text. **Nit / wiring note.**
- **Where:** `templates/types/reoffer.ts` (every `price`).
- Nothing in the type stops a caller passing a maximum bid. The domain already prices the offer at the
  placed bid (RA6). Suggest renaming the var to `placedBidPrice`, or documenting that it must be the
  stored offer price formatted, so the wiring PR can't take it from `maxBid`.

### F7. Fact cards run together in plain text. **Info (existing behaviour).**
- The text part shows `LotLot 12: Blue StudyOffer price£1,200Answer by17:00 on ...`. This comes from the
  shared `components/FactCard.tsx` table and affects every existing email that uses it, so it is not
  caused by this PR. The paragraphs, terms, button URL, help and footer all come through in the text
  part. A follow-up could add a plain-text separator to `FactCard`.
