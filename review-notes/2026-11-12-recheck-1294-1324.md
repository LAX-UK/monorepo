### Both features intact

- **Devices list (#1309).** `listForSubject` still returns `country`, `clientIds` (live, unconsumed refresh tokens across the lineage) and `isCurrent`. The service still filters staff and mobile client ids out of `products`.
- **Device-aware new-device email and forget-on-sign-out (#1314).** All three paths survive and are wired:
  - `AuthHookDeps` and `AuthEnv` carry `sessionExpiry`, `readDeviceHash` and `forgetDeviceMarker` side by side.
  - `server.ts` passes all three to the hooks.
  - `create-auth-issuer.ts` sets the clamp and both device functions.
  - The `create.after` hook sends `deviceHash` to `securityNotices.notify`.
  - The `delete.after` hook forgets the marker on `/sign-out` only, and swallows errors.
  - The repository's `deleteSession`, `deleteAllSessions` and `deleteOtherSessions` (sign out one, all, others) each call `retireRevokedBrowserMarkers`.
  - The `session-hooks.test.ts` resolution keeps both the expiry-hook and delete-hook describe blocks complete.

### signedInAt: nullable handling is honest

- Main had `signedInAt` and `lastSeenAt` as `Date | null` and fell back in the service: `session.signedInAt ?? session.createdAt`.
- The resolution moves the fallback into SQL:
  - `signedInAt = coalesce(signed_in_at, created_at)`
  - `lastActiveAt = coalesce(last_seen_at, updated_at)`
- These are the same expressions the lifetime rules, the sweep and the clamp use (`session-lifetime.sql.ts`). So the non-null `Date` type is true, and the devices list shows the same times that decide when a record ends. The nullable fields were removed from the port rather than kept twice.
- The integration test now asserts the fallback for an old record (`signedInAt: null` → `createdAt`, `lastSeenAt: null` → `updatedAt`).
- Subject export (from main) still reads the raw nullable `last_seen_at`, so it reports `null` where the devices list shows the fallback. This is not a security issue: the export is honest about what was stored.

### No auth bypass

- The update hook still refuses a renewal that clamps to or before now (`return false`).
- `create.before` still clamps, still runs the suspended-subject check and still refuses social sign-in for staff.
- `IDENTIFY_ONLY` (`disableRefresh`) is still used on every identify-only read: the OIDC routes, the 2FA authorize gate, the passkey policy, device sessions, the request handler and the app.
- Lifetimes and caps are as specified:
  - clients: 7d idle, 30d absolute, 20 browsers;
  - staff: 12h idle, 24h absolute, 5 browsers.

### Migration ordering

- 0365 now sits last in the journal (`when` 1809604800000), after main's 0359 and 0360.
- The 0365 test reads the time from the journal instead of hard-coding it.
- **Nit:** if main adds a migration with a smaller `when` before this merges, drizzle would skip it on any database that already applied 0365. Re-check the journal tail at merge time.

## B. PR #1324 — fixer commits since `638f9025`

Fixer commits: `1512856d1` (B1–B3), `97e4f99fe` (N1), `8bb27c7f3` (N2), `a17761172` (N3 and the copy). `471c6f53f` is a plain merge of main.

### B1 `GET /sales` preview — fixed

- For non-preview viewers, `SaleListReadService` passes preview lots through `withPublicLotResults`, and the reader is wired in `create-lot-catalog-services.ts`.
- `mapLotToSummary` recomputes `hasWinner` from the now-null `winnerId`. A pending lot therefore shows `currentPrice` = starting price, `hasWinner: false`, `resultPending: true` and no `checkoutPricing`, both before and after re-award.
- Staff skip the presenter and the reader call.
- The unused `listSalesForPublicApi` gets the same treatment.

### B2 `/users/me/watchlist` and B3 `/users/me/bids` — fixed

- `attachEnrichedLots` now calls `presentLotsForViewer`:
  - Lots that are not the viewer's go through `withPublicLotResults`, then `withoutBuyerIds`, which nulls `winnerId` and `buyerLegalEntityId` and keeps a recomputed `hasWinner`.
  - A lot is the viewer's when `winnerId === userId`, or its `buyerLegalEntityId` is one of the accounts the viewer bid for here: the personal list takes these from the viewer's own bid rows, the account list uses that account.
- Pending lots return before checkout pricing is computed. Invoices stay scoped to the viewer.
- Result:
  - A watcher or losing bidder sees a pending lot as ended, with the starting price, no ids and no pricing, before and after re-award.
  - The new buyer sees their own lot at the offer price, with invoice or checkout pricing.
  - Non-pending lots that are not the viewer's also lose the buyer ids. This closes the dashboard part of the old `winnerId` leak.
- `watching.vm.ts` maps `resultPending` to the "Ended" standing, with figure "–" and label "ended".
- My bids (`myBidStatus`) shows a pending lot that is not the viewer's as "not-won". This is true for that viewer and leaks nothing. A lot won through an org the person bid for still reads "won" (tested).
