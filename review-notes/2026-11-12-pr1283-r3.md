## Verdict: **FIX FIRST** (one small blocking fix)

All three findings from round 2 are fixed or acceptably resolved. On a 15-minute poller that runs on time, the brute-force run over every Intl zone, every step and every day in 2026–27 finds **zero** violations. One gap remains, and I'd fix it before merge: nothing at send time enforces the step window. The schedule now puts 2,346 sends within 30 minutes of the window's close. So a single late or missed poll (a deploy, a busy worker, a Redis blip) sends that reminder on the next London day. For `due_day`, that means after the due time, which breaks a hard requirement of the plan. The fix is a few lines (F-1 below).

## New findings

### F-1 (Medium, **blocking**): no close-of-window guard at send time, so a late poll sends on the wrong London day or after the due time
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/services/buyer-default/reminder-recipient-schedule.ts:24` (only checks `reached === step`), `packages/domain/src/payment-reminder.ts:150-158` (`paymentReminderOpen` keeps returning a step until the *next* step's London day opens). Deal payment reminders take the same path (`packages/domain/src/private-sale-deal/deal-reminder.ts:51`). `reminderClosesAt` is used only inside `reminderSendAt`, never at send time.
- **Scenario:** Adelaide recipient, `due_day`, due 2026-03-29. Send time sits less than 30 min before the close (brute force: **2,346** of 909,150 zone×step×day slots in 2026–27 have under 30 min of slack, and 21,534 more have under 60 min). The 23:45 London poll is late or missed (deploy restart, BullMQ backlog). The next poll at 00:00 sees `due_day` still "open", because `overdue` doesn't open until due+3, and the recipient's step is still `due_day`. The reminder is staged after the due time, on the next London day. The same applies to `before_due`, which stays open through due−1, and to `overdue`, which stays open indefinitely (up to the 90-day query age). On main, sends sat at 09:00 London with about 15 h of slack. This PR deliberately moves sends to `closes − 15 min`, so one poll's jitter now breaks the invariant.
- **Fix:** In `stageForRecipientsDue`, treat `now > reminderClosesAt(step, dueAt)` as "window closed". Don't stage. Count the recipient as done, so the step gets claimed and is never sent late (or claim the step outright once its window has closed). Add a unit test: an Adelaide/Brisbane recipient with `now = closes + 1 ms` → nothing staged, and the step is claimed. Optionally move `latest` to `closes − 2 × interval` for one poll of tolerance.

### F-2 (Medium, non-blocking, partly pre-existing): candidate query has no lower bound, so stale rows can starve the 200-row batch
- **Where:** `packages/persistence/src/repositories/drizzle-payment-reminder.store.ts:54-65`.
- **Scenario:** The PR adds only an upper bound (`dueAt ≤ now + 3 days`). A payment with any step never claimed keeps `count(*) < 3` and stays a candidate for 90 days, e.g. `before_due` skipped because the buyer was on a compliance hold that day. Rows are ordered `createdAt asc` with `limit 200`, so 200 or more such rows fill every batch: `openStepFor` returns `null` for each, and newer invoices are never reached. This is a "miss", which the brute force can't see because it assumes every candidate is listed. `drizzle-private-sale-deal-reminder.store.ts:84-86` (untouched by this PR) has no window or count filter at all: `invoiced`/`paid` deals with long-past deadlines are listed forever, ordered by `due asc`.
- **Fix:** Bound to open windows: `dueAt > now − 4 days` (the `overdue` window ends at the end of London day due+3), or exclude rows whose latest-open step is already claimed. Do the same in the deal store (`deadline.due > now() − 4 days`). This also makes F-1's late-send path for `overdue` unreachable after day +4.

### F-3 (Low): saved placeholder zones (`UTC`, `Etc/*`) are scheduled on one clock and rendered on another
- **Where:** `packages/persistence/src/repositories/drizzle-recipient-zone.reader.ts:30` (`isValidZone` keeps `UTC`/`Etc/*`) vs `packages/email/src/recipient-clock.ts:19` (`normaliseViewerZone` drops them).
- **Scenario:** A directory row with `UTC` gets the reminder at 09:00 UTC (10:00 BST), but the email shows London only, with no footer. That's harmless, but it contradicts "no saved zone = official only / 09:00 London". Account normally stores place zones only (`normalisePlaceZone`), so this needs a legacy or synced value.
- **Fix:** Use `normaliseViewerZone` (or `normalisePlaceZone`) in the reader, so both sides treat placeholders as "no zone".

### F-4 (Info): fallback sends are not at 09:00 local
24,174 of 915,420 (2.6%) recipient×step×day cases can't fit 09:00 local inside the London day minus one poll interval. They are sent at London midnight instead, which is 09:00–13:45 local for UTC+10…+12:45 zones. This is the documented trade-off: the London day takes precedence over 09:00. It's fine, but the PR description and the plan should say so explicitly.

## Other checks (no issue found)
- **Double sends around midnight/DST at 15-min cadence:** 0 duplicates. Each recipient is staged under `payment_reminder:<payment>:<step>:<recipient>` (deals: `private_sale_payment_reminder:<deal>:<step>:<recipient>`), so re-staging is a no-op. Steps are claimed by insert-if-absent (`onConflictDoNothing`), and only after every recipient is staged. A zone change mid-day cannot produce a second send, because the key is per step.
- **Hourly repeat removal:** `removeRepeatable` for `hourly-payment-reminders` before adding the 15-min job. Good. BullMQ `every` aligns to epoch multiples, so polls land at :00/:15/:30/:45 UTC.
- **Bad saved zone → London:** reader drops invalid zones with a one-time warn (logs the zone string only, no user id). Integration test `drizzle-recipient-zone.reader.integration.test.ts` covers `Mars/Olympus_Mons`. Security notices use `effectiveAccountTimeZone` → London.
- **PII:** the new logs carry no user ids or emails. The billing-inbox copy is rendered with an official-only clock, so the member's zone isn't exposed to the organisation inbox.
- **Email copy:** official time with zone first ("Thu 12 Nov, 18:00 GMT"). The local line appears only when the saved zone differs, with the day named only when it differs ("Fri 13 Nov, 05:00 in Sydney" vs "20:00 in Cairo"). Footer "<City> time comes from your LAX Account. Change time zone" with the link, shown only when a local time was shown. No saved zone or London → official only. Snapshot tests cover the watched-lot reminder, lot and deal payment reminders, and the digest. Collection reminders stay at 09:00 London (`dealReminderToSend` with a `null` zone). Due date stays end of London day (`invoiceDueAt`).

## Brute-force results
Script: simulates the worker's 15-minute poller through `PaymentReminderService` + `stageForRecipientsDue` semantics (open step, per-recipient `reminderStepReached`, outbox-key dedupe, claim-when-all-staged) using the built `@auction/domain`. Each case has two recipients (the zone + a no-zone/London payer). Due = end of London day. Every London step day 2026-01-01…2027-12-31 × 3 steps × all 418 `Intl.supportedValuesOf("timeZone")` zones (+ Europe/London). The poll loop runs from 1 h before `before_due` opens to 3 days after `overdue` opens. The cheap mirror was checked against the real `paymentReminderOpen`/`reminderStepReached` on 1.67 M polls (10 edge zones, every 3rd day): **0 mismatches**.

| Poll phase | Cases | Wrong London day | Before opens | After due on due day | Duplicates | Misses | Fallback (London-midnight) sends |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| :00/:15/:30/:45 (BullMQ aligned) | 915,420 | **0** | **0** | **0** | **0** | **0** | 24,174 |
| +14 min (worst-case on-cadence lag) | 915,420 | **0** | **0** | **0** | **0** | **0** | 24,174 |

Slack to window close (`reminderClosesAt − reminderSendAt`), 2026–27, all zones: under 30 min in **2,346** slots, 30–60 min in 21,534, out of 909,150. In those slots one late or missed poll gives a wrong-day / after-due send (F-1).
