### Owner question: what commission rate does settlement use?

**Plain answer:** settlement does **not** use the signed `consignment_agreement.commission_percent`. It uses the seller organisation's `legal_entity.platform_fee_bps`, which defaults to **5%** when unset. It charges that rate on the **buyer's whole invoice total, hammer plus buyer's premium**. The seller is then paid that total less the fee. So the seller receives the buyer's premium, and no VAT is charged on the commission.

Evidence (head of `bs1-reoffer-seller`; settlement code is the same as `main`):

1. **The rate.** `packages/finance-runtime/src/platform-fee.policy.ts`:
   - line 4: `DEFAULT_PLATFORM_FEE_BPS = 500`
   - lines 29-36: the rate is `legalEntities.findById(seller).platformFeeBps`, otherwise 500. The fee is `round(total × bps / 10,000)`.
2. **What the fee is charged on.** `packages/validators/src/invoice-lines.ts:61`: `totalPence = hammerPence + premiumPence`. Lines 35-36 say no VAT or ARR lines are produced yet.
3. **The fee is stored on the payment when it is raised:**
   - `apps/api/src/services/payment/create-pending-for-winner.pipeline.ts:296-299`: `amountPence = priced.totalPence`, then `computePlatformFeeFromPence(sellerLegalEntityId, amountPence)`.
   - `packages/finance-runtime/src/lot-invoice-initiation.service.ts:161,191`: the same.
4. **The payout uses the stored fee.** `packages/finance-runtime/src/payout/payout-settlement.ts:60,72` builds the sale line from `payment.amount` and `payment.platformFee`. `packages/domain/src/selling-records.ts:90,94` then gives `net = gross − fee share`.
5. **Settlement never reads the agreement rate.** `commission_percent` (`packages/db/src/schema/consignment-agreements.ts:26`) is read only by:
   - the agreement itself (`consignment-agreement.service.ts`, `agreement-document.service.ts`)
   - its PDF
   - submissions
   - the seller-facing views of the agreement

   There are no references in `packages/finance-runtime`, `apps/api/src/services/payment` or the payout code.

**Money impact (for the owner).** Every seller is paid on a rate the agreement does not control. Suppose a seller signs a 15% + VAT commission agreement. A lot sells at £82,000 hammer with a 25% buyer's premium, so the buyer pays £102,500.

| | Under the signed agreement | What settlement pays today |
|---|---|---|
| Fee | 15% of £82,000 = £12,300, plus VAT £2,460 | 5% of £102,500 = £5,125 |
| Seller receives | about £67,240 | £97,375 |
| LAX keeps | £14,760 plus the £20,500 buyer's premium (about £35,260) | £5,125 |

So LAX underpays itself by about **£30,135 on that one lot**, and the buyer's premium is passed to the seller.

The reverse can also happen: a seller who negotiated below 5% is overcharged. This affects every payout, not just re-offers. This PR only makes the existing behaviour visible: the seller page now shows "Buyer's premium +£X" as part of the seller's proceeds.

Recommendation: the owner and finance should decide which rate is the source of truth before this panel goes live to sellers. If it is the agreement, change the settlement fee policy, not this PR. That means:
- read the lot's agreement `commission_percent`
- charge it on the hammer
- treat VAT on commission
- keep the buyer's premium for LAX

`sellerNetForSale` would then follow automatically, because it calls the same policy.

## #1319: sweep missed-invoice hardening: SHIP

### A. A cancelled payment never causes a re-invoice: FIXED

`drizzle-reoffer-sweep.reader.ts:99-116`: any payment by the offered account on the lot hides the row, whatever its status, with one exception: `status = 'cancelled' AND bid_invoice.voided_at IS NOT NULL`.

- **Why the exception is safe.** The only writer of `voided_at` is `drizzle-buyer-default.repository.ts:145`, the buyer default.
- **What the left join does.** A cancelled payment with no invoice still hides the row, which fails safe.
- **Refunded payments** also hide the row, as `ensureForLot` would refuse them anyway.
- **The earlier review's scenarios:**
  - relinquished win: not listed
  - stale checkout cancelled, then captured late: not listed, because the invoice is still live
  - plain stale cancel: not listed
- **Integration tests** (`reoffer-clock.integration.test.ts:791-830`) cover:
  - a live-invoice cancel
  - a cancel with no invoice
  - a refund
  - a default-voided cancel, which is listed
  - another account's payment, which is ignored
- **Grants.** `worker_app` already reads `bid_invoice.voided_at`. The role contracts pass.

### B. Recoverable vs terminal reasons: FIXED

`packages/domain/src/reoffer-invoice-outcome.ts`:
- **Terminal** is an explicit allowlist, the one the earlier review asked for.
- **Retried with a 24h back-off:** everything else, including the recoverable set, `unchanged` and unknown reasons. The marker is `invoice_not_raised:retry:<reason>`.
- **Listing.** The reader re-lists retry marks once `updated_at` is older than the back-off.
- **Marker.** It never overwrites a terminal mark, but it does replace a retry mark, which restarts the back-off.
- **Runbook.** It documents the re-arm SQL (`docs/runbooks/monitoring-alerts.md`).
- **Test.** The integration test at `:867` covers the full cycle: retry mark, re-listing after the back-off, and the invoice raised once the cause is fixed.
- Two harmless notes:
  - `LIKE 'invoice_not_raised:retry:%'` treats `_` as a wildcard. Only these markers exist, so nothing else can match.
  - After a successful retry the old retry marker stays in `close_reason`. Nothing reads it for an accepted offer, and the payment now hides the row.

### C. Alert before or with the mark: FIXED

`apps/worker/src/jobs/reoffer-sweep.ts:104-120` calls `log.warn` and `Sentry.captureMessage` first, then `markInvoiceNotRaised`. The test "reports before it marks, so a crash at the mark cannot lose the report" (`reoffer-sweep.test.ts:181`) pins the order.
