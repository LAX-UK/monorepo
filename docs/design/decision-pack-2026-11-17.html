1. ZI-5: Per-consignor agreement data for stock moves

Today

"Offer at auction" in LAX Admin needs a required checkbox, "The consignor agreed to sell at auction". History records only "Consignor agreement confirmed" and who clicked. "Offer on lax.art" asks nothing. LAX holds no record of who consigned a Zoho original, or on what terms.

Problem

Nobody can answer "what did this consignor agree to, until when, at what minimum?", and the question is asked again on every move.

Options

A. Checkbox plus evidence noteNot recommended

Like "Grant sale authority". One small PR. Still free text, per move.

The consignor agreed to sell at auction
What the agreement is based on
Signed letter of 3 March

B. Agreements per consignorRecommended

Agreements on the client's page; each original has a Consignor. The move dialog reads them.

SaleSpring works on paper
Starting price£1,800
Covered by agreement with Example Gallery Ltd, signed 3 Mar 2026, LAX Bid and lax.art, until 2 Mar 2027.
Below the lowest price agreed (£2,000).
Make draft lot
No agreement covers LAX Bid for Example Gallery Ltd.
Add agreementMake draft lot

C. Reuse Bid's consignment agreementNot recommended

Keyed to a Bid submission and seller conditions; Zoho originals have neither.

PR plan (B)

  1. S: migration (agreement, artwork consignor link with history), rollback, contracts.
  2. M: Agreements section on the client page (add, retire, file), capabilities, audit.
  3. M: Consignor field on the From Zoho record; optional column and filter.
  4. S: move dialogs read the agreement; checkbox removed after the transition.

2. LA-4: From Zoho "missing price" not surfaced

Today

Zoho's price is stored as information and shown only inside the move dialogs ("Not set in Zoho"). The record's "What Zoho says" has no price row. "Still missing" counts image, size and medium only, by design: the LAX price is set when a work is offered.

Problem

Staff learn Zoho has no price only when they open a move dialog, and cannot list unpriced works.

Options

A. Zoho's price as informationRecommended

A row on the record and an optional, sortable list column. No tone, not a gap.

Size50 × 70 cm
Price in Zoho£4,800.00
Price in ZohoNot set in Zoho

B. "Price on request" stateNot recommended

Lets a Zoho omission decide what buyers see. "Enquire" is already a deliberate staff choice.

Price on request

C. WarningNot recommended

"Still missing: price" implies Zoho must be fixed first, which is not required.

Still missing: image, price

PR plan (A)

  1. S: record row, optional column with sort, list item field, tests, screenshots.

3. GAP-49: Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link at basket and checkout

Today

Basket → Details (your details, delivery) → Stripe's hosted page, where wallets already appear if on in Stripe. The Shop loads no Stripe.js.

Problem

Wallet users type everything on our form before they see the wallet. Baymard counts long or complicated checkouts among the top reasons for abandonment and recommends express options early, beside guest checkout.

Options

A. Hosted page onlyNot recommended

No code. Wallets stay one step late.

B. Express Checkout ElementRecommended

On basket and Details, on Checkout Sessions with Elements; same webhook. UK delivery and collection in the sheet; hosted page kept.

Total£245.00
Checkout
Or pay with
Apple PayGoogle PayLink
Express checkout
Apple PayLink
Or continue below
Your details
Full name

C. Embedded Payment ElementNot recommended

All payment on our page. Larger change; not needed to bring wallets forward.

Changes to the checkout design (CO1–CO18)

  • Basket summary: wallet row under the ink primary button; wallet buttons keep their own brand styles, no Shop accent.
  • Details: express row first, form unchanged below, for guests and signed-in shoppers.
  • Steps: a wallet payment goes straight to Confirmation; Payment shows as done.
  • International quote path: no wallet row.
  • Needs: Shop domains registered as payment method domains in Stripe; Stripe.js allowed in the Shop's security headers.

PR plan (B)

  1. S: domain registration, security headers, flag off by default.
  2. M: shop-api Elements session, UK rates, hold on confirm, contract tests.
  3. M: Shop UI on basket and Details, states, light/dark/375, e2e in Stripe test mode.
  4. S: production switch-on after device checks.

Q4 wallets on basket too? [Yes.] Q5 collection in the sheet? [Yes, zero-price rate.]

5. GAP-53: Client workspace extras

Today

After sending a work: "Sell another work" opens a blank wizard. Each work is its own submission. Drafts have no delete. The register's second action is not in the repository; this pack takes it to be "Delete draft" (Q8).

Options: another work ("Duplicate last")

A. "Sell another like this"Recommended

Pre-fills artist, category, medium, edition, signed and provenance. Not title, photos, size, prices or condition.

Sell another like thisSell another work
Started from Harbour at dusk. Check every step before you send it.

B. Multi-work groupsNot recommended

Changes submission, agreement, lot and payout models.

C. "Add another" on reviewNot recommended

Mid-wizard branching is easy to lose.

Options: delete draft

A. "Delete draft" actionRecommended

Row menu and the draft's page; confirm; soft delete, purged after 30 days.

View consignment
Sell another like this
Delete draft
Delete this draft?

Its photos and answers are removed. This cannot be undone.

CancelDelete draft

B. Drafts expire after 90 daysNot recommended

Work disappears without warning.

PR plan

  1. S: "Sell another like this" source, button and menu item.
  2. M: delete draft API (drafts only), soft delete migration, purge job.
  3. S: delete draft UI, confirm, toast "Draft deleted".

Q7 copy photos for a series? [No.] Q8 is "Delete draft" the register's second action? [Yes.]
