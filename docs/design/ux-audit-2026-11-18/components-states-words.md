# UX audit 2026-11-18: components, states and words

Read-only audit of the code on `origin/main` (Wed 18 Nov 2026). It covers the public products: LAX Bid (public site and client workspace, not Bid admin), LAX Shop, lax.art, LAX Account, the hosted sign-in pages (`packages/auth/src/hosted-auth`) and the shared kits they draw on (`packages/ui`, `packages/ui/src/lax`, `packages/marketing-ui`, `packages/branding`, `packages/email`, `packages/datetime`, `packages/shop-portal/vm`). It compares the same element across products, using the rules in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/FORMS.md`, `docs/ui/form-controls.md` and `docs/design/decision-pack-2026-11-17.md` (sentence case, one accent per product, the form-error rule, dates through `@auction/datetime`). Nothing was rendered. Rows marked "verify on test" need a browser or device check. Items already known (LAX Admin favicons, Account favicons, the staff top bar mark, `/favicon.ico`) are left out.

**Top 3 problems**

1. **Fields zoom the page on iPhone, and the same field is built five ways.** The LAX kit's `TextInput`, `TextArea` and `NativeSelect` render 14px text at every width, and the shadcn `Textarea` does too, so iOS zooms in on focus. This hits Bid contact, newsletter, register to bid and private sales, lax.art enquiries and Shop's checkout country select. Fields are built as lax `Field` (Bid), `MarketingFormField` with a `shop-field` skin (Shop) or an underline skin (Art), hand-wired shadcn (Account) and hosted HTML. As a result, heights (36 or 44px), error position, optional marking and checkboxes differ from product to product (CSW-1 to CSW-3, CSW-14 to CSW-22).
2. **The form-error rule is broken on many forms with two or more fields.** None of the following show an error summary: hosted sign-in and sign-up, Account's 9-field address book, Bid sign-up onboarding, organisation details, register to bid, telephone booking and absentee bids. Some show the error only in a banner or a toast, or let the browser's own bubbles fire first. Money fields suggest "e.g. £5,000", but typing "5,000" saves £5 (CSW-4 to CSW-13).
3. **States and feedback are not one family.** Account has no `global-error.tsx` and no loading state at all. Bid's 404s use two designs and voices. Shop, Art and Account never report boundary errors. Only Bid has an offline banner. Toasts follow three specs (top-centre 6s, bottom-right 5s, none in Shop or lax.art). Overlays come from seven dialog or sheet families, status chips from two components, empty states from two components. Date-only and money styles differ between products, with "Sept", "UK time", "(London time)", "500£ - 5,000£" and four forms of one lot estimate (CSW-23 onward).
