# UX audit 2026-11-18: identity and shell

Read-only audit of the code on `origin/main` (Wed 18 Nov 2026). It compares the same element across LAX Bid (public site, client workspace and Bid admin), LAX Shop, lax.art, LAX Account, LAX Admin, the hosted sign-in pages and the shared kits. Nothing was rendered: rows marked "verify on test" need a check in a browser. These are already known and are left out: LAX Admin favicons redirect to sign-in when signed out; LAX Account has no `public/favicons`; the staff shell top bar has no LAX mark; `/favicon.ico` returns 404 on every host.

**Top 3 problems**

1. **Bid names the wrong company in its identity surfaces.** The installed-app name, the logo alt text on every Bid header, footer and sign-in logo, the About page, the /legal page and the email-verified notice say "London Auction House Ltd" or "London Art Exchange". Bid's legal entity is London Auction Xchange LTD (IDS-1 to IDS-4).
2. **Page titles follow four patterns.** Bid, Account and LAX Admin use "Page · Product". Shop and lax.art use "Page | Product", and several Shop pages carry only "LAX Shop". Bid admin uses "Page · Admin · LAX", so with both staff tools open the tabs read "Orders · Admin · LAX" next to "Orders · LAX Admin" (IDS-5 to IDS-9).
3. **The signed-in Bid workspace and Bid's sign-in and onboarding pages still use the legacy raster-era logo files, not the lockup kit.** Bid task pages have no logo at all, and the organisation workspace has no footer, legal links or family links on phones. The Bid app therefore looks like a different family from its own public site and from Shop, lax.art and Account (IDS-12 to IDS-16, IDS-33).
